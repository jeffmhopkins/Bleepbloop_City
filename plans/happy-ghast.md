# Happy ghast: flying mount and sky platform

**When:** Phase 5. A stretch goal on the first Nether trip ([Session 6](../progress/session-plan.md#session-6-the-nether-gateway)), and a real goal once the Nether is routine.
**Goal:** Raise a happy ghast at the base, harness it, and use it as a flying platform for the [grand builds](building-goals.md) and as a 4-seat sightseeing mount.
**Status:** ⬜ Not started. Plan only.

The facts below were checked on the Minecraft Wiki ([Happy Ghast](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Happy_Ghast), [Dried Ghast](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dried_Ghast), [Harness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Harness), [Nether Fossil](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fossil), [Bartering](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bartering), [Transportation](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Transportation), 2026-10-07) and the official [Bedrock 1.21.90 changelog](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-1-21-90-bedrock-changelog). Background on the rest of the new content is in [what's new](../notes/whats-new.md).

## Why it's worth it here

- **Build platform:** you can stand on top of a happy ghast in midair, and **it holds still while a player is on it**. It snaps to face a cardinal direction (north, south, east or west), so getting on and off is easy. That gives you scaffolding at any height for the oxidized-copper towers and spires, the lighthouse/watchtower and the storage hall roof, with no pillars to clean up afterward.
- **Friends:** up to **4 players** ride at once. The first rider steers.
- **Airlift:** carries boats and big leashed mobs (horses, donkeys, mules, camels, sniffers) on a lead. Mobs can ride in a carried boat, but **players can't**: the lead snaps.
- **Early flight:** elytra are end-game loot (End ships, after the dragon). A happy ghast only needs a Nether trip.

## What it isn't

- **Not fast.** A ridden happy ghast flies at about **3.6 m/s**, slower than walking (4.3 m/s) and sprinting (5.6). Compare an average horse (about 9.5 m/s) and elytra (about 30 m/s gliding, 33.5 with rockets). For distance, use horses, ice-boat roads, the Nether, and later elytra.
- **Not fire-proof,** unlike a regular ghast. Keep it away from lava.
- **One only:** it can't be bred, so each happy ghast needs its own dried ghast.

## Steps

### 1. Get a dried ghast (Nether)
- **Best early source: soul sand valleys.** Every **Nether fossil** (the big bone-block skeletons poking out of the soul sand) has a **1-in-3** chance of a dried ghast next to it. It breaks instantly with anything and always drops itself.
- **Bartering:** each gold ingot given to a piglin has about a **2.1%** (10 in 469) chance of returning a dried ghast. That's roughly 47 gold for one on average, so it's a side bonus rather than a plan.
- **Crafting:** **8 ghast tears around 1 soul sand.** Ghast tears drop from ghasts, which usually float over lava, so this is a later option.
- Soul sand valleys have a lot of skeletons and ghasts. Go with full gear and stay near the portal (see the [Session 6](../progress/session-plan.md#session-6-the-nether-gateway) safety notes).

### 2. Rehydrate it (Overworld, at the base)
- **Waterlog it:** place it in water or pour water onto it. It goes through 3 visible stages over about **20 minutes**, then turns into a **ghastling**.
- If the water is removed, it slowly dries back at the same rate. **Breaking it resets it to dry.**
- Do this where you're working so the area stays loaded. Leave room for the adult: it's a **4×4×4-block** mob. Where it lives is Jeffrey's call.

### 3. Grow the ghastling
- On its own: **20 minutes.**
- **(BE): 10 snowballs grow a new ghastling to an adult** right away. Snow is next to spawn: a shovel on snow blocks or layers gives snowballs (they stack to 16).
- It follows players (and many passive mobs) within 16 blocks, and roams within 32 blocks of its home.
- **Don't feed it a golden dandelion,** which pauses growth (unless you want a permanent baby).

### 4. Harness it
- **Harness:** 3 leather in the top row, then glass, wool, glass in the middle row. The wool sets the color, and it can be re-dyed later.
- Leather competes with books (enchanting) and item frames (labels and the wall map). Budget **3 leather** from the cow pen.
- Use the harness on the adult to equip it (a dispenser works too). Shears take it off, but only when nobody is riding.

### 5. Fly
- Forward goes where you're looking. **Jump** goes straight up. There's **no down key**: look down and press forward, or look up and press back.
- **Dismounting puts you on top of it.** It stays put while you stand there, which is your build platform.
- Lure it with **snowballs**, or with a harness if it isn't wearing one. A lead stretches **16 blocks** on a happy ghast (12 on other mobs).
- **Home:** an unharnessed happy ghast roams within 64 blocks of its home; harnessed (or a ghastling), within 32. Home resets to its current spot when you dismount, add or remove the harness, detach a lead, or stop tempting it. So it wanders around **wherever you last got off**. Park it on a lead at a post, or dismount where you want it to stay.
- **Healing:** normally 1 health every 30 seconds. At cloud height (**Y 187–196**), or in rain or snow with open sky above, it heals 1 health per second. It has 20 health.
- A name tag (1 paper + 1 nugget) gives it a name.
