# Phase 3 — Pick the real site

**When:** Before you build walls
**Goal:** Find a site you'll still want in a month.
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

Walk until you find most of these within a couple of minutes of each other.

## Site checklist

- [ ] Flat or gently sloped ground, not a swamp or a dark forest
- [ ] A village, or at least a plains/meadow/river edge (villagers become your trading hall later)
- [ ] Surface water
- [ ] A cave entrance or a ravine
- [ ] Trees
- [ ] Ideally a second biome next door (desert for sand, or a cold biome if you want poplars)

### Avoid

- [ ] Not on a stronghold
- [ ] Not inside a woodland mansion
- [ ] Not on the camp you already looted

## Tasks

- [ ] Mark the site with a banner or a tall dirt pillar
- [ ] Record the coordinates in [notes/coordinates.md](../notes/coordinates.md)
- [ ] Strip-mine or branch-mine from here, not from the starter shack
- [ ] Give the new site a door
- [ ] Give the new site light
- [ ] Give the new site a chest
- [ ] Move the bed (only after the door, light, and chest are in)
