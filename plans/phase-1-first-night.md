# Phase 1 — First night

**Goal:** Do not die.
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

Do this before you decorate anything.

## Tasks

### Tools
- [ ] Punch a tree
- [ ] Make a wooden pick
- [ ] Mine stone
- [ ] Make a stone pick
- [ ] Make a stone axe
- [ ] Make a stone sword

### Materials
- [ ] Gather about a stack of logs
- [ ] Gather 20–30 cobble
- [ ] Get wool: kill sheep or find an abandoned camp

### Shelter and sleep
- [ ] Dig a 1×2 hole in a hillside, or pillar up if you're in a bad biome
- [ ] Light it
- [ ] If there's no real bed yet, use a straw bed to skip the night
- [ ] Sleep in a real bed as soon as you have 3 wool and 3 planks, so your spawn is set

### Optional: abandoned camp
- [ ] Raid a nearby abandoned camp, then leave (do not build on it)

## Tips

- **Straw beds:** three hay bales craft four straw beds. One use each, they skip the night, and they do not move your spawn.
- **Abandoned camps** (18 biomes, including meadows and cherry groves) are free early loot: copper gear, buckets, compasses, and sometimes iron, diamonds, or explorer maps. Raid one if it's close, then leave. Log its location in [coordinates](../notes/coordinates.md).

## Done when

- [ ] I have a stack of food
- [ ] I have a stone tool set
- [ ] I have a chest
