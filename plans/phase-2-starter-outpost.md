# Phase 2 — Starter outpost

**When:** Days 1–3
**Goal:** A shack, not a home. Put it near spawn or your first bed so you can find it.
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

## Tasks

### Minimum kit
- [ ] Crafting table
- [ ] Furnace
- [ ] Two chests
- [ ] A door
- [ ] Torches every 7–8 blocks

### Farm
- [ ] Till a 9×9 plot of dirt with a water source in the middle
- [ ] Fence it
- [ ] Plant wheat
- [ ] Add carrots or potatoes once you find them

Bread and baked potatoes carry you until you have a cow pen.

### Animal pen
- [ ] Pen with two cows minimum
- [ ] Add sheep
- [ ] Add chickens
- [ ] Breed only when you have extra food

### Mining
- [ ] Mine a staircase down to around Y=16 for iron and coal
- [ ] Do not chase diamonds yet. Iron armor and an iron pick change the game more than a pretty roof.

### Iron upgrades
- [ ] Iron pick
- [ ] Iron tools
- [ ] Iron armor
- [ ] Smoker (cuts cook time in half)
- [ ] Blast furnace (cuts smelt time in half)

## Done when

- [ ] I have iron tools and armor
- [ ] I have a food farm that refills itself
- [ ] I have a chest sorted into tools, food, and blocks
