# Phase 2 — Starter outpost

**When:** Days 1–3
**Goal:** A shack, not a home. Put it near spawn or your first bed so you can find it.
**Status:** see the [tracker](../progress/tracker.md). Checkboxes below are this phase's task list.

### Farm
- [ ] Till a 9×9 plot of dirt with a water source in the middle (partial: a starter patch tilled right beside the water instead, since there's no bucket yet)

Bread and baked potatoes carry you until you have a cow pen.

### Animal pen
- [x] Build the pen (2026-10-07; empty so far)
- [ ] Pen with two cows minimum (no cows yet: lead them in with wheat once it's ready)
- [ ] Add sheep
- [ ] Add chickens
- [ ] Breed only when you have extra food

### Mining
- [ ] Mine a staircase down to around Y=16 for iron and coal (Session 3 went into natural caves instead and found iron and coal there; no staircase mine reported)
- [ ] Do not chase diamonds yet. Iron armor and an iron pick change the game more than a pretty roof.
- [x] Smelt the **copper ore** you pass, too (Session 3: a bunch of copper, made into armor). Copper tools mine the same blocks as stone, but faster and with more durability.
