# What's new: catching up after a 2-year break

**Server: Bedrock 26.50, Wilderness Bound** (the matching Java release is 26.3). Jeffrey last played around **October 2024**. This guide covers every game drop since then, oldest first. Each item says what it is, why it matters for this world (spawn has a mountain, a cherry grove, big caves, ocean, and ice and a cold biome nearby), and how to get it.

Everything here was checked on the [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki) drop and item pages (2026-10-07). Happy ghast and Wilderness Bound details were also cross-checked against the official [1.21.90](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-1-21-90-bedrock-changelog) and [26.50](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft--bedrock-edition-26-50-changelog) Bedrock changelogs. Anything that couldn't be confirmed was left out. Bedrock-vs-Java differences are flagged **(BE)** or **(JE)** where they matter.

## Top things that change how you play

1. **Happy ghasts: a flying mount for up to 4 players, and a platform you can stand on in midair.** You raise one from a **dried ghast** found in the Nether. It's slow (about 3.6 m/s, slower than walking) but makes great scaffolding for the grand builds. Plan: [plans/happy-ghast.md](../plans/happy-ghast.md).
2. **Copper is now a gear tier:**
   - **Copper tools** mine the same blocks as stone (so no diamonds or redstone), but faster and with more durability.
   - **Copper armor** (10 armor points) sits between leather (7) and iron (15). It's a real stopgap, because copper ore is everywhere in the staircase mine.
3. **Bundles:** 1 string + 1 leather makes a bundle that holds a stack's worth of *mixed* items in one slot. Good for torches, seeds, flowers and loot.
4. **Mounts and pets got cheaper:**
   - **Saddles** are craftable: 3 leather + 1 iron.
   - **Leads** are 5 string, no slimeball.
   - **Name tags** are craftable: 1 paper + 1 nugget of any kind.
   - **Shears** remove saddles, harnesses and leads.
5. **New night dangers:**
   - **Creakings** in pale gardens. They're unkillable; break their creaking heart instead.
   - **Zombie horsemen** with spears in plains and savannas.
   - **Camel husk jockeys** and **parched** (skeletons shooting Weakness arrows) in deserts.
   - Zombies, husks and piglins can now spawn holding a **spear**, which has 4.5-block reach.
6. **Copper golems sort items.** They carry items out of a copper chest into matching chests. This is the [storage gallery](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md) showpiece, and abandoned camps can hand you a free oxidized golem statue.
7. **Abandoned camps and straw beds** (Wilderness Bound) matter in the first week. Camps give free early loot and explorer maps. Straw beds skip the night without moving your spawn.
8. **Locator bar:** in multiplayer, friends show as colored markers on the HUD where the XP bar was. On Bedrock the game rule is `playerwaypoints` (`everyone` or `off`; the default is `everyone`). It shows where friends are without swapping coordinates.
9. **Lodestones are cheap now:** 8 chiseled stone bricks + **1 iron ingot** (it used to take netherite). A compass used on one points to it, even in the Nether. That's handy for marking the portal or the village.
10. **Animals look different by climate.** Spawn is next to a cold biome, so expect **cold cows, pigs and chickens**, and mostly **black sheep** in cold biomes. Cold chickens lay **blue eggs**, which hatch into cold chicks.

## Just before the break (skip if you played these)

**Tricky Trials** (June 2024, Bedrock 1.21.0) and **Armored Paws** (April 2024) came out before October 2024:
- **Trial chambers:** underground structures with **trial spawners**, **vaults** (opened with trial keys), and **breezes** that drop **breeze rods** and shoot **wind charges**.
- The **mace** (heavy core + breeze rod), the **crafter**, copper bulbs and other new copper and tuff blocks, and **bogged** (poison skeletons).
- **Armadillos** (their scutes make wolf armor) and 8 **wolf variants**.

## Bedrock vs Java: the differences that matter here

| Topic | Bedrock (this server) | Java |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growing a ghastling with snowballs | 10 snowballs and a new ghastling is grown | Each snowball cuts 10% of the remaining time |
| Player markers on maps | Only **locator** maps (made with a compass) | Any map |
| Banner markers on maps | Not available | Use a map on a banner |
| Creaking spawns | Night only | Night and thunderstorms |
| Spear | Shared jab cooldown across spears; no offhand | Attack cooldown; offhand charge allowed |
| Locator bar game rule | `playerwaypoints` (`everyone`/`off`) | `locator_bar` (true/false) |
| Shelves and redstone | Activate only on certain redstone ticks | Normal |
| Vibrant Visuals | Available | Not yet |
| Flying machines and pistons | Use Bedrock designs ([flying machine](../plans/flying-machine.md)) | Different piston behavior |
