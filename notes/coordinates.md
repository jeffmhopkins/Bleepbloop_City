## Navigating

- **Read X and Z off the screen** to head for any row above: compare your numbers with the target's. On the coordinate grid, X grows to the east and Z grows to the south ([Coordinates](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coordinates)). Y is height, so it's your depth gauge underground.
- Home base is planned near world spawn, so a plain compass (4 iron ingots + 1 redstone dust) points home. No lodestone needed for home.
- **Lodestones are cheap now** (Spring to Life): 8 chiseled stone bricks + **1 iron ingot**. Use a compass on one and that compass points to it, in the Overworld, the Nether or the End (same dimension only). That's good for the village, the portal, or the Nether-side portal ([Lodestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lodestone)).
- **Locator bar:** in multiplayer, other players show as markers on the HUD (game rule `playerwaypoints`).
- The sun and moon rise in the east and set in the west.
- **Local-area map** (planned for Sessions 4–6; see the [map thread](../progress/session-plan.md#map-thread-a-local-area-map)). Hang a map in an item frame by the base door or storage entrance and carry clones.
  - Make it at a cartography table: 1 paper + a compass gives a **locator** map, which shows your position. Maps made from paper alone don't.
  - Use it, then hold it while walking; it draws the surface around you. Zoom it out (1 paper per level) **before** filling, because zooming wipes the drawing. Level 0 covers 128×128 blocks; level 2 covers 512×512.
  - Maps snap to a fixed world grid, so same-zoom maps from neighboring areas tile into one wall picture. A framed locator map shows a green marker for its frame on every clone, which points you home.
  - On Bedrock, using a map on a banner doesn't add a marker (that's Java-only), and maps don't mark spawn or lodestones, so the map is for seeing the area and the table above is for exact spots. Note which map shows each place (for example "base map") next to its coordinates.
- Use tall torch-topped pillars, banners at junctions, and torch trails kept on one side.
- A recovery compass (echo shards) points to your last death.
- Later: lit roads or ice-boat roads, and a marked Nether hub. Nether X and Z are the Overworld's divided by 8; Y doesn't scale ([Nether Portal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Portal)).
