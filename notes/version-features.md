# Version features: Wilderness Bound

**Java 26.3 / Bedrock 26.50.** The new bits that matter for this plan.

| Feature | What it's for | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straw beds | Three hay bales craft four straw beds. One use each, they skip the night, and they do not move your spawn. Handy for travel because they do not steal your spawn point back at the base. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md), [Multiplayer](../plans/multiplayer-server.md) |
| Abandoned camps | Found in 18 biomes, including meadows and cherry groves. Free early loot: copper gear, buckets, compasses, and sometimes iron, diamonds, or explorer maps. Raid, then leave; do not build on one. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md), [Phase 3](../plans/phase-3-pick-the-site.md) |
| Copper gear | Matters in the first week; one of the things abandoned camps give you. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md) |
| Wool and concrete stairs and slabs | Colored trim that is not just full blocks. Wool stairs also dampen sound. | [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |
| Cushions | Seats. Put them at a table; they are not structural. | [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |
| Poplars | Found in cold biomes. Stone bricks plus poplar is one of the suggested palettes. | [Phase 3](../plans/phase-3-pick-the-site.md), [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |
