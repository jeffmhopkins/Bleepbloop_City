# Version features: Wilderness Bound

**Java 26.3 / Bedrock 26.50.** The new bits that matter for this plan.

| Feature | What it's for | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straw beds | Three hay bales craft four straw beds. One use each, they skip the night, and they do not move your spawn. Handy for travel because they do not steal your spawn point back at the base. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md), [Multiplayer](../plans/multiplayer-server.md) |
| Abandoned camps | Found in 18 biomes, including meadows and cherry groves. Free early loot: copper gear, buckets, compasses, and sometimes iron, diamonds, or explorer maps. Raid, then leave; do not build on one. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md), [Phase 3](../plans/phase-3-pick-the-site.md) |
| Copper gear | Matters in the first week; one of the things abandoned camps give you. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md) |
| Wool and concrete stairs and slabs | Colored trim that is not just full blocks. Wool stairs also dampen sound. | [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |
| Cushions | Seats. Put them at a table; they are not structural. | [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |
| Poplars | Found in cold biomes. Stone bricks plus poplar is one of the suggested palettes. | [Phase 3](../plans/phase-3-pick-the-site.md), [Building style](../plans/building-style.md) |

## Copper golems and copper chests

Not new in Wilderness Bound (they arrived in The Copper Age drop: Java 1.21.9 / Bedrock 1.21.111), but central to the [storage and sorting](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md) big goal. Checked against the [Copper Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Golem), [Copper Chest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Chest), and [Copper Golem Statue](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Golem_Statue) wiki pages.

| Feature | What it's for | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper golems | Built from a block of copper + a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern (pumpkin last). Takes up to 16 items from copper chests and puts them in wooden or trapped chests that are empty or already hold that item. Oxidizes over time and turns into a statue unless waxed with honeycomb. | [Storage and sorting](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md) |
| Copper chests | 8 copper ingots around a chest. The pick-up point for golems. Oxidizes; wax with honeycomb, scrape by sneaking with an axe. | [Storage and sorting](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md), [Phase 4](../plans/phase-4-the-base.md) |
| Copper golem statues | What an unwaxed, fully oxidized golem turns into. Scrape with an axe back to unoxidized, then once more to revive it. | [Storage and sorting](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md) |
| Abandoned camp copper (new in 26.3 / 26.50) | Oxidized copper chests and oxidized copper golem statues now generate in abandoned camps: a free early golem. | [Phase 1](../plans/phase-1-first-night.md), [Storage and sorting](../plans/storage-and-sorting.md) |
